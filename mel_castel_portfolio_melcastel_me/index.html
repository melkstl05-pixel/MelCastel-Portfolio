05ABOUT

From the
island outward.

Mel Castel is a Martinican multidisciplinary artist and musician working across sound, image, movement and curation.

Her practice moves between Martinique, London and wider diasporic spaces, drawing from ancestral rhythms, natural environments, storytelling and experimental forms.

Music remains at the centre: a way to connect, heal, remember and listen.
